What is evidence-based, and what remains uncertain?

Wang et al. 2015, Table 1 supplies published saRNA protocol guidance. It does not establish universal requirements or validate this app's ranking. Equal preference points and thermodynamic tie-breaking are project choices.

Optional criteria from Reynolds et al. 2004 and Ui-Tei et al. 2004 come from siRNA knockdown: transfer to activation is an extrapolation. Project-derived hypotheses are labelled exploratory; unsourced rules have no traceable support.

No preset has demonstrated predictive benefit in the bundled sequence-only benchmark. Missing context is unknown, not a negative result. A predicted guide, motif match, CpG island or BLAST alignment does not establish activation in your experimental cell. Inspect each rule's source and evidence scope before interpreting a pass or exclusion.

File format and strand notation

Columns: name, sequence, role, direction, sense, antisense, source. Use sense and antisense columns for supplied duplexes. Mark terminal overhangs as [dT][dT] or [UU]; unmarked bases are part of the core. RNA U becomes DNA T for matching; reverse complementation also reverses direction and pairs U with A. Unspecified partners and overhangs remain inferred or unknown.

How rules and evidence work

Evidence labels describe support in the cited context, not validated saRNA efficacy. A gate excludes candidates that fail it. A preference adds one point when met. Off removes the check. Edits stay a draft until you run Python again. Continuous asymmetry is a tiebreak, and core length defines candidate generation.
